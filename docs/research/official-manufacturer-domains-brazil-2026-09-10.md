# Official manufacturer sources for Brazil — research snapshot, 2026-09-10

This is a **preferred search seed catalog**, not an allowlist. Its 65 canonical entries identify official brand/manufacturer or national representative websites relevant to Brazilian vehicle research. A missing brand, domain, CDN, page or PDF must never prevent research. Search the open public web when seeds are insufficient, including useful secondary sources such as Webmotors, and retain the source URL and provenance. A domain classification describes where a source came from; it does not guarantee that every claim on a page is correct.

Scope: passenger cars, pickups, light commercial vehicles/vans, trucks, buses and bus bodies. Motorcycles, agricultural/construction equipment, charging-only businesses, fleet rental companies and independent gray imports are outside the catalog. Sources for announced entrants and historical model documentation remain useful; inclusion does not certify that every model is currently sold or that its specifications match the requested model year.

The companion JSON preserves exact entry URLs and a few model path templates observed on official sites. It is a research snapshot for integration, not an independently enforced policy or the application's source of truth. Aliases are search-routing conveniences. Related brands that share a site need not become the same canonical manufacturer.

## Method and coverage

Cross-check the domestic manufacturer landscape against [ANFAVEA's company roster](https://anfavea.com.br/site/associates/) and [current industrial footprint](https://anfavea.com.br/institucional/sobre/), then verify national brand pages directly. Expand electric and commercial coverage with [ABVE's member roster](https://abve.org.br/nossos-associados/), filtering out suppliers, fleet lessors and two-wheel businesses. Neither association is a complete inventory of all brands; ANFAVEA groups several brands under corporate members and also includes machinery outside scope. ABEIFA's website could not be retrieved during this run, so no claim of completing its current roster is made; official manufacturer pages, including premium brand dealer locators, supply direct evidence instead.

Research included current national pages, explicit corporate links, manufacturer launch announcements and technical-document links. Some sites returned 403, a bot challenge or a timeout. Where another first-party or association source established the exact destination, that distinction is recorded below. This is a broad verified catalog, **not a proof that every manufacturer or historical technical document in Brazil has been enumerated**.

## Document examples and why open discovery matters

- **BYD Shark:** the [official Brazilian model page](https://www.byd.com/br/car/shark) links a [current technical PDF](https://www.byd.com/material/__CN/byd-site/br/fichas-tecnicas-2026/update-13-07-2026/07-13-2026---ficha-txiunica/BYD_Shark_V2.pdf). The [manufacturer launch announcement dated 25 October 2024](https://www.byd.com/br/noticias-byd-brasil/chega-ao-brasil-a-byd-shark---primeira-caminhonete-super-hibrida1) is separate dated evidence. A current PDF must not silently be presented as definitive model-year-2024 data.
- **Geely EX5:** the [official model page](https://www.geelybrasil.com.br/ex5) directly links [its technical PDF on static.autoforce.com](https://static.autoforce.com/plugins/files/clientes/geely-brasil/produtos/ws/ficha-tecnica-geely-ex5-v2.pdf). The shared host is expected infrastructure, and a hostname allowlist would unnecessarily block this valid document. Record the parent official page as the provenance chain. The implementation agent also exercised live discovery and reported this PDF as READABLE without registering its host.
- **Mercedes-Benz:** the official cars site links [a technical noise/emissions table](https://www2.mercedes-benz.com.br/content/dam/brazil/passengercars/informative/noise-opacity/tabela-ru%C3%ADdo_veiculos_Mercedes-Benz.pdf) and [a road-safety manual](https://www2.mercedes-benz.com.br/content/dam/brazil/passengercars/being-an-owner/manual-seguranca-transito.pdf) under its own www2 host. File format alone does not establish that a document is a model specification.
- **Jetour:** an indexed [official S06 technical PDF](https://jetourbr.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/AFB_FICHA-TECNICA-S06_DM_V1.pdf) is hosted on jetourbr.com. The similar jetour.com.br returned a directory listing and is not the preferred seed.
- **Neta:** the [official press page](https://netaauto.com.br/imprensa/) exposes technical-sheet links, and a [Brazilian X owner's manual](https://netaauto.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/X-Manual-do-usuario-2025-02.pdf) provides dated first-party material even when present commercial status is uncertain.

Document links are examples observed at a point in time, not exhaustive lists or permanent URLs. Follow current links, redirects, public CDN destinations and useful secondary reporting. Keep public-network/SSRF protections separate from source preference; accepting a public source does not authorize accessing private addresses.

## Gaps, name collisions and nonblocking follow-up

- **Bentley, Sinotruk, Dongfeng/Forthing and Seres:** this run did not establish a current official Brazil entry point with sufficient first-party evidence. Do not invent domains or infer official national distribution from used listings, independent imports or a foreign-market catalog. These remain searchable through the open web and can be added to preferred seeds once verified.
- **JMEV / Emova:** [E-Motors Brasil](https://emotorsbrasil.com.br/) publishes Emova/JMEV vehicle pages, but this run did not establish its exclusive or official manufacturer-representation status. It remains a useful external lead, not a confirmed official seed. Do not confuse it with the unrelated [ABVE E-Motors member](https://abve.org.br/associado/12190-2/), whose profile covers two/three-wheel mobility and a different website.
- **Arrow Mobility:** [ABVE](https://abve.org.br/associado/arrow-mobility/) confirms its electric-van project, but the manufacturer's exact website entry was not fetched in this run. Leave as a nonblocking follow-up rather than guessing from its contact email.
- **Cadillac:** first-party Brazilian launch and official national website are established; the homepage still describes vehicles as coming soon. Search results and future catalog updates must not be treated as proof of immediate retail availability.
- **MG:** preferred mgmotoroficial.com.br presents the national range; mgmotor.com.br returned an “Em breve” placeholder. **Foton:** fotondobrasil.com.br presents vehicles; foton.com.br is an unrelated multimedia-hardware business. Exact verified URLs avoid these name collisions.
- **CAOA, Ferrari and Eletra retrieval limitations:** source ownership can be established through corporate/association links even if live page extraction is blocked. Search official indexed documents first, then credible secondary pages with explicit provenance. Failure to fetch is not evidence that specifications do not exist.

## Application behavior this research supports

1. Search verified official national seed pages and links first, while checking the requested brand, model, market and year against the actual content.
2. If those sources do not answer the question, broaden to the public web. Prefer reputable secondary sources and distinguish their provenance in results; Webmotors is an example, not another exhaustive list.
3. Follow public document/CDN links even when their host is absent from this catalog. Preserve the official parent page when a manufacturer linked the document.
4. Report a missing seed as a lookup gap, never as “manufacturer has no approved domains” or “no official specification exists.”
5. Preserve year conflicts and source uncertainty. Official branding alone cannot resolve contradictory values or a mismatch between publication date and model year.

## Implemented policy and validation

The application registry is `apps/ai/src/mastra/ingestion/manufacturer-registry.ts`. All 65 researched entries and their exact website URLs are present. Existing model-path templates and aliases were preserved. `SPECSYNC_INGESTION_SOURCE_DOMAINS` now overrides discovery hints only: it cannot deny a source URL, a PDF link or a public redirect destination.

Discovery checks preferred manufacturer websites first, then performs open-web official-source search and, when usable evidence remains missing, a secondary-source search. The secondary-source examples in the agent instructions are Webmotors, iCarros, Quatro Rodas and Autoesporte; results are not restricted to those sites. Unknown brands still reach web search. Public HTTPS HTML/PDF capture keeps its existing download, timeout and private-network protections.

Each candidate records whether it came from a known manufacturer website, was linked from one, or came from an external website. Offsite manufacturer links preserve `linkedFrom`. These categories are shown in the chat card and remain separate from applicability, which is always unverified at discovery time. Curator review and publication rules are unchanged.

This exercised real downloads with the web-search callback intentionally returning no results; no CDN registration was needed.

The full AI suite passed 320 tests; the web suite passed 280 tests and its architecture suite passed 65 checks.
